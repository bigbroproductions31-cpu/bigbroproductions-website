Shiyad is an entrepreneur who is lovingly known as Bhai among friends and family. He has multiple businesses in real estate and gold. He's also a financer and producer in Bollywood movies and has done 4 projects till now. A key aspect to highlight is that he only invests in meaningful scripts and does not encourage purely commercial films. He is also very particular about the director and the core team creating the project. His upcoming films are aimed at providing chances to young budding film makers. Family dramas and action movies are now in focus genres for him. He encourages differentiated cinematography and looks to bring innovative storytelling methods into his productions.
